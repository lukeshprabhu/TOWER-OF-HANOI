About Tower of Hanoi

Tower of Hanoi is a free, beautifully designed online puzzle game that brings the classic mathematical challenge to your browser. Whether you're a student learning recursion, a casual puzzle lover, or a competitive speedrunner — this game is for you.

📚 Educational Value

The Tower of Hanoi is one of the most important problems in computer science. Our game includes comprehensive educational content covering:

  • The history of the puzzle (invented by Édouard Lucas in 1883)
  • The mathematical formula: T(n) = 2ⁿ − 1
  • The recursive algorithm with pseudocode
  • Step-by-step solving strategies and tips

🛠️ Technology

Built with modern web technologies — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no external frameworks. The backend uses Python (Flask) with MySQL for the leaderboard system.
