Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tower of Hanoi ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you submit a score to the leaderboard, we collect your name and country selection. This is voluntary.
  • Game Data: We collect game statistics such as number of moves, time taken, and disk count for leaderboard purposes.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information about your device, browser type, IP address, and browsing behavior through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, personalization, and advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the game and leaderboard functionality
  • To display your scores on the public leaderboard
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings.
  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data to help us understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts (Outfit) for typography.

5. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential: Theme preference, cookie consent choice
  • Analytics: Understanding user behavior and site performance
  • Advertising: Serving personalized ads via Google AdSense

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or by using our cookie consent banner.

6. Data Retention

Leaderboard data is retained as long as the score remains in the top 100 for its category. If your score is surpassed, it may be automatically removed. Usage data collected through analytics is retained according to the respective service provider's policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdraw consent for cookies

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@yourdomain.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@yourdomain.com.
